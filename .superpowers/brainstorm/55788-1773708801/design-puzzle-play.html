Key changes to puzzle play screen:
  • Hint fix: hintCount from PuzzleSession drives board highlights directly — taps no longer erase hint
  • Progressive hints: 2 levels, 1 credit each (from-square → full move)
  • AppBar context: Shows chapter name + puzzle number in chapter + credits
  • Status bar: Clear "Your turn" / "AI thinking" indicator
  • Solved banner: Inline at bottom with credits earned (replaces modal bottom sheet)
  • Wrong move: Inline error bar instead of icon-only feedback
